MIMAROPA · PHILIPPINES

An island with a story

Small island. Big-hearted welcome.

Set between the Bondoc Peninsula and Mindoro, Marinduque is often called the heart of the Philippine archipelago. Its curved coastline leads to fishing towns, forested slopes, and beaches made for unhurried days.

Come for the scenery; stay for the warmth, the local flavors, and a culture that feels distinctly its own.

Tradition, still alive

A festival you feel as much as see.

Every Holy Week, the Moriones Festival brings masked Roman-soldier characters into the streets of Marinduque. The island's towns each add their own energy to this vivid retelling of the story of Longinus.

Between festival days, look for the everyday culture too: warm conversations, community celebrations, and local treats like crisp uraro arrowroot cookies.
